A singing engineer. Developer at the production team [musicolare](https://musicolare.com), and lead engineer of the video streaming platform [initium ; auditorium](https://www.initium-auditorium.com). Works on web and app development with React and React Native, and backend development on AWS.

Active in vocal ensembles, mainly choirs, and a member of many groups. Selected member of the [World Youth Choir](https://www.worldyouthchoir.org/) 2020. Selected member of the 5th–6th and 9th–11th [JCA Youth Choir](https://jcanet.or.jp/event/jca-youth/JCAyouth-concept.htm). Founder and director of [Shirube Choir](https://shirube-choir.com). Member conductor of [Chor OBANDES](https://chor-obandes.jimdofree.com/).
